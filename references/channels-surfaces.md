# Channels and Owned Surfaces

## Surface registry

Inventory every place a lifecycle treatment can appear:

- email
- text messaging
- push
- in-product messaging
- website personalization
- account and settings pages
- checkout and post-transaction surfaces
- service communication
- customer-support outreach
- paid media
- direct mail
- high-touch outreach
- loyalty or subscription surfaces

## Surface metadata

Capture:

- surface name
- customer need supported
- lifecycle suitability
- targeting capability
- real-time or scheduled support
- personalization capability
- intrusiveness
- measurement capability
- owner
- approval and quality process
- implementation effort
- standard lead time
- availability
- deprecation risk

## Portfolio classification

Use neutral portfolio buckets:

- low effort, high proven opportunity
- high effort, high proven opportunity
- low effort, low proven opportunity
- high effort, low proven opportunity
- unproven and testable
- unavailable

Fill high-value cells first using:

**surface reach × transition value × targeting confidence × feasibility**

## Channel guidance

- Use in-product treatment when the customer can act immediately.
- Use email for detail, critical information, and re-engagement.
- Use push or text only when immediacy justifies interruption.
- Use paid media for acquisition, anonymous return, lapsed reinforcement, and suppression after recovery.
- Use human outreach for complex, high-value, or high-risk cases.
- Use direct mail selectively when physical presence adds meaningful value.

## Churn-adjacent surfaces

Billing, account-management, and cancellation surfaces may receive passive, prioritized support or save treatments. Do not create campaigns whose purpose is to drive customers to a churn-adjacent surface.

## Surface governance

- publish ownership and lead times
- publish creative and accessibility constraints
- instrument before launch
- use feature flags for risky or launch-sensitive placements
- maintain an exposure calendar
- document ineffective surfaces
- require a kill or revision rule for unproven placements
